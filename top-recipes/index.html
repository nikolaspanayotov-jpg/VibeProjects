FROM YOUR WEEK, TO YOUR BASKET

Your shopping list.

Amounts follow your household size, meal portions and linked leftovers. Untick anything you already have or want to skip, then use − and + or type a quantity. Only selected items with a quantity above zero appear in your screenshot.

How amounts are worked out

Choose your household size beside the plan, or keep the recipe’s stated servings. Use a meal’s options to change its portions or link leftovers to an earlier cooking. Leftover servings are added to that original batch. Shared ingredients are combined when their units match; grams and kilograms, or millilitres and litres, are converted together.

Whole items round up. “To taste”, pinches and unspecified amounts start at one pack to check in your cupboard. Different foods and incompatible units stay on separate lines.

Your choices and quantities save for this week. If a plan change alters an ingredient total, its calculated quantity refreshes; your tick choices stay the same.
