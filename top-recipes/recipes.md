# Favourite recipes

This is the editable recipe collection for Top Recipes. The dish names are your starting favourites; the quantities and methods below are suggested starter versions, not a record of your personal recipes. Replace them freely with the versions you enjoy most.

To add a recipe, copy a complete recipe section from its `##` heading to the end of its notes, then change its title and permanent `id`. Keep the metadata fields, Ingredients table columns and Method heading. Use `breakfast`, `lunch` or `dinner` in `meal_types`; more than one is allowed. Keep ingredient IDs unique within each recipe. Optional ingredients should say “(optional)” in their name.

Each recipe's quantities make its stated number of servings. Times are estimates; `wait_minutes` includes unattended waiting such as chilling or resting. The website design is in [design.md](design.md).

## Overnight oats

```yaml
id: overnight-oats
meal_types: [breakfast]
servings: 1
prep_minutes: 5
cook_minutes: 0
wait_minutes: 480
tags: [make-ahead, no-cook, vegetarian]
```

Creamy oats prepared the night before, with yoghurt, berries and a little honey. A simple breakfast to take straight from the fridge.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rolled-oats | 40 g | Rolled oats | Oats |
| milk | 100 ml | Milk or unsweetened plant milk | Oats |
| yoghurt | 75 g | Plain Greek yoghurt | Oats |
| chia-seeds | 1 tsp | Chia seeds (optional) | Oats |
| honey | 1 tsp | Honey or maple syrup (optional) | Oats |
| berries | 80 g | Fresh or frozen berries | Topping |

### Method

1. Stir the oats, milk, yoghurt, chia seeds and honey together in a jar or covered container. Leave a little room for the oats to expand.
2. Cover and refrigerate for about 8 hours, or overnight.
3. Stir in the morning. Add a splash of milk if you prefer a looser texture, then top with the berries.

### Notes

- The starter timing includes an 8-hour overnight chill, so plan this breakfast the evening before.
- For a dairy-free version, use plant milk and plant yoghurt. Use maple syrup for a vegan version.
- Contains oats and, with the suggested dairy ingredients, milk. Use certified gluten-free oats if needed and check product labels.

## Chicken and leek pie

```yaml
id: chicken-and-leek-pie
meal_types: [lunch]
servings: 4
prep_minutes: 20
cook_minutes: 55
wait_minutes: 10
tags: [comfort-food, family-favourite, batch-cooking]
```

Tender chicken and softened leeks in a creamy sauce, finished with a golden puff-pastry lid. Serve with green vegetables or a simple salad.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chicken | 500 g | Boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces | Filling |
| leeks | 2 medium | Leeks, trimmed, washed and sliced | Filling |
| butter | 25 g | Butter | Filling |
| olive-oil | 1 tbsp | Olive oil | Filling |
| garlic | 2 cloves | Garlic, finely chopped | Filling |
| flour | 25 g | Plain flour | Filling |
| chicken-stock | 300 ml | Chicken stock | Filling |
| double-cream | 100 ml | Double cream | Filling |
| dijon-mustard | 1 tsp | Dijon mustard | Filling |
| thyme | 1 tsp | Dried thyme | Filling |
| salt | To taste | Salt | Seasoning |
| black-pepper | To taste | Ground black pepper | Seasoning |
| puff-pastry | 1 ready-rolled sheet, about 320 g | Puff pastry | Pastry lid |
| egg | 1 | Egg, beaten for brushing | Pastry lid |

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C, or 180°C fan. Have a roughly 24 cm pie dish ready.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Brown the chicken for about 6–8 minutes, working in batches if needed, then transfer to a plate. It will finish cooking in the sauce.
3. Lower the heat to medium, add the butter and leeks, and cook for about 8 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
4. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually add the stock, stirring to make a smooth sauce. Add the cream, mustard and thyme, then return the chicken and its juices to the pan.
5. Simmer gently for about 10 minutes until the sauce thickens and the chicken is cooked through. Check the largest pieces reach 75°C in the centre. Season to taste.
6. Transfer the filling to the pie dish and let it cool for about 10 minutes. Place the pastry over the dish, trim and crimp the edges, and cut a small steam vent in the centre. Brush with beaten egg.
7. Bake for 25–30 minutes until the pastry is puffed and golden and the filling is piping hot.

### Notes

- The 55-minute cooking estimate includes the hob and oven stages; actual time depends on the pan and oven.
- The filling can be prepared ahead, cooled promptly and refrigerated. If baking from a chilled filling, allow extra time and check the centre is piping hot before serving.
- Suggested sides are serving ideas and are not included in the ingredient quantities above.
- Contains wheat/gluten, milk, egg and mustard in the suggested ingredients; check stock and pastry labels too.

## Moussaka

```yaml
id: moussaka
meal_types: [dinner]
servings: 6
prep_minutes: 25
cook_minutes: 80
wait_minutes: 15
tags: [comfort-food, batch-cooking, weekend-cooking]
```

A comforting baked dish of roasted aubergine, a warmly spiced lamb and tomato sauce, and a creamy béchamel topping. This starter version uses aubergine without a potato layer.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| aubergines | 3 large | Aubergines, sliced into rounds about 1 cm thick | Aubergine layer |
| olive-oil | 3 tbsp | Olive oil, divided between roasting and the sauce | Aubergine layer and sauce |
| lamb-mince | 500 g | Lamb mince | Meat sauce |
| onion | 1 large | Onion, finely chopped | Meat sauce |
| garlic | 2 cloves | Garlic, finely chopped | Meat sauce |
| chopped-tomatoes | 400 g | Tinned chopped tomatoes | Meat sauce |
| tomato-puree | 2 tbsp | Tomato purée | Meat sauce |
| cinnamon | 1 tsp | Ground cinnamon | Meat sauce |
| oregano | 1 tsp | Dried oregano | Meat sauce |
| butter | 50 g | Butter | Béchamel |
| flour | 50 g | Plain flour | Béchamel |
| milk | 600 ml | Milk | Béchamel |
| parmesan | 50 g | Parmesan, finely grated | Béchamel |
| egg | 1 | Egg, beaten | Béchamel |
| nutmeg | 1 pinch | Ground nutmeg | Béchamel |
| salt | To taste | Salt | Seasoning |
| black-pepper | To taste | Ground black pepper | Seasoning |

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C, or 200°C fan. Arrange the aubergine slices on baking trays, brush with 2 tablespoons of the olive oil, season lightly and roast for 25–30 minutes, turning once, until tender and lightly browned.
2. While the aubergine roasts, heat the remaining tablespoon of oil in a large pan. Cook the onion for about 5 minutes, add the garlic for 1 minute, then add the lamb. Cook for about 8 minutes, breaking it up until browned.
3. Stir in the tomato purée, cinnamon and oregano. Add the chopped tomatoes and simmer uncovered for about 20 minutes until thick, stirring occasionally. Season to taste.
4. Make the béchamel while the sauce simmers: melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the flour and cook for 1–2 minutes. Gradually whisk in the milk and cook for about 8 minutes until smooth and thickened.
5. Take the béchamel off the heat and let it cool for about 5 minutes. Stir in the Parmesan and nutmeg, then whisk in the beaten egg. Season lightly.
6. Reduce the oven to 200°C, or 180°C fan. In a roughly 30 × 20 cm baking dish, layer half the aubergine, the meat sauce and the remaining aubergine. Spread the béchamel over the top.
7. Bake for 35–40 minutes until the top is golden and the centre is piping hot. Rest for 15 minutes before cutting into portions.

### Notes

- The cooking estimate assumes the aubergine, meat sauce and béchamel are prepared in parallel, as described. Allow longer if making each component one after another.
- Beef mince can replace lamb if preferred.
- Contains wheat/gluten, milk and egg in the suggested ingredients.

## Chicken with rice

```yaml
id: chicken-with-rice
meal_types: [dinner, lunch]
servings: 4
prep_minutes: 15
cook_minutes: 50
wait_minutes: 5
tags: [one-pot, family-favourite, oven-baked]
```

Golden chicken thighs baked with fluffy rice, onion and a gently seasoned stock. A comforting meal from one dish.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chicken | 600 g | Boneless, skinless chicken thighs | Main |
| rice | 300 g | Long-grain white rice, rinsed | Main |
| onion | 1 large | Onion, finely chopped | Main |
| garlic | 2 cloves | Garlic, finely chopped | Main |
| olive-oil | 2 tbsp | Olive oil | Main |
| chicken-stock | 700 ml | Hot chicken stock | Main |
| paprika | 1 tsp | Sweet paprika | Seasoning |
| thyme | 1 tsp | Dried thyme | Seasoning |
| salt | To taste | Salt | Seasoning |
| black-pepper | To taste | Ground black pepper | Seasoning |
| parsley | 1 small handful | Fresh parsley, chopped (optional) | To finish |

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C, or 180°C fan. Season the chicken with the paprika, thyme and a little salt and pepper.
2. Heat the oil in a large ovenproof casserole over medium-high heat. Brown the chicken for 6–8 minutes, then lift onto a plate.
3. Lower the heat and cook the onion in the same casserole for about 5 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute, then stir in the rinsed rice.
4. Pour in the hot stock, stir and bring to a simmer. Place the chicken on top and cover tightly with a lid or foil.
5. Bake for about 30 minutes, then uncover and cook for another 5–10 minutes, until the rice is tender, the liquid is absorbed and the chicken reaches 75°C in the centre. Add a splash of hot water if the rice needs longer.
6. Rest, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff the rice around the chicken and scatter with parsley if using.

### Notes

- This version uses white rice; brown rice needs more liquid and a longer cooking time.

## Cottage pie

```yaml
id: cottage-pie
meal_types: [dinner, lunch]
servings: 4
prep_minutes: 20
cook_minutes: 65
wait_minutes: 10
tags: [comfort-food, family-favourite, batch-cooking]
```

A rich beef and vegetable filling under a golden layer of buttery mashed potato. A familiar favourite for a cosy evening.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| beef-mince | 500 g | Beef mince | Filling |
| onion | 1 large | Onion, finely chopped | Filling |
| carrots | 2 medium | Carrots, diced | Filling |
| garlic | 2 cloves | Garlic, finely chopped | Filling |
| olive-oil | 1 tbsp | Olive oil | Filling |
| tomato-puree | 2 tbsp | Tomato purée | Filling |
| beef-stock | 300 ml | Beef stock | Filling |
| worcestershire-sauce | 1 tbsp | Worcestershire sauce | Filling |
| thyme | 1 tsp | Dried thyme | Filling |
| peas | 100 g | Frozen peas | Filling |
| potatoes | 1 kg | Floury potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks | Mash |
| butter | 40 g | Butter | Mash |
| milk | 100 ml | Milk | Mash |
| cheddar | 50 g | Cheddar, grated (optional) | Topping |
| salt | To taste | Salt | Seasoning |
| black-pepper | To taste | Ground black pepper | Seasoning |

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C, or 180°C fan. Heat the oil in a large pan and soften the onion and carrots for 8–10 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and beef mince. Cook for about 8 minutes, breaking up the mince, until browned.
3. Stir in the tomato purée, stock, Worcestershire sauce and thyme. Simmer uncovered for about 20 minutes until the filling is thick, then stir in the peas and season.
4. Meanwhile, put the potatoes in a pan of cold salted water. Bring to the boil and simmer for 15–20 minutes until tender. Drain, let the steam escape, then mash with the butter and milk.
5. Spoon the beef filling into a roughly 24 cm baking dish. Spread the mash over it, roughen with a fork and scatter with the Cheddar if using.
6. Bake for about 25 minutes until golden on top and bubbling around the edges. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

### Notes

- The cooking estimate assumes the potatoes cook while the filling simmers.
- Cottage pie uses beef; lamb mince makes a shepherd's pie variation.

## Lasagna

```yaml
id: lasagna
meal_types: [dinner, lunch]
servings: 6
prep_minutes: 25
cook_minutes: 80
wait_minutes: 15
tags: [comfort-food, pasta, batch-cooking]
```

Layers of beef and tomato sauce, pasta and creamy béchamel, baked until golden. A generous dish for sharing.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| beef-mince | 500 g | Beef mince | Meat sauce |
| onion | 1 large | Onion, finely chopped | Meat sauce |
| carrot | 1 medium | Carrot, finely diced | Meat sauce |
| garlic | 2 cloves | Garlic, finely chopped | Meat sauce |
| olive-oil | 1 tbsp | Olive oil | Meat sauce |
| chopped-tomatoes | 800 g | Tinned chopped tomatoes | Meat sauce |
| tomato-puree | 2 tbsp | Tomato purée | Meat sauce |
| oregano | 1 tsp | Dried oregano | Meat sauce |
| butter | 50 g | Butter | Béchamel |
| flour | 50 g | Plain flour | Béchamel |
| milk | 600 ml | Milk | Béchamel |
| nutmeg | 1 pinch | Ground nutmeg | Béchamel |
| lasagna-sheets | 250 g | Dried lasagna sheets that need no precooking | Layers |
| parmesan | 75 g | Parmesan, finely grated | Layers |
| salt | To taste | Salt | Seasoning |
| black-pepper | To taste | Ground black pepper | Seasoning |

### Method

1. Heat the oil in a large pan. Soften the onion and carrot for about 8 minutes, add the garlic for 1 minute, then add the mince. Cook for about 8 minutes until browned.
2. Stir in the tomato purée, tomatoes and oregano. Simmer for 20–25 minutes until thick but still spoonable. Season to taste.
3. While the meat sauce simmers, melt the butter in a saucepan. Stir in the flour and cook for 1–2 minutes. Gradually whisk in the milk and cook for about 8 minutes until smooth and thick. Add the nutmeg and a little seasoning.
4. Heat the oven to 200°C, or 180°C fan. Spread a little meat sauce in a roughly 30 × 20 cm baking dish. Add a layer of pasta, then meat sauce and béchamel. Repeat, finishing with béchamel, and scatter Parmesan between layers and over the top.
5. Bake for 35–40 minutes, or according to the pasta packet, until the sheets are tender, the centre is piping hot and the top is golden. Cover loosely with foil if it browns before the pasta is ready.
6. Rest for 15 minutes before slicing.

### Notes

- Keep enough sauce around every pasta sheet so it can soften as the lasagna bakes.
- The cooking estimate assumes the béchamel is prepared while the meat sauce simmers.

Spaghetti with a rich beef, tomato and vegetable sauce. An easy favourite for a generous midweek supper.

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| beef-mince | 500 g | Beef mince | Sauce |
| onion | 1 large | Onion, finely chopped | Sauce |
| carrot | 1 medium | Carrot, finely diced | Sauce |
| celery | 1 stick | Celery, finely diced | Sauce |

- The sauce can simmer longer over a low heat; add a splash of water if it becomes too thick.

Tender baked salmon with lemon, garlic and dill. A fresh, simple option for a quick lunch or dinner.

## Chicken soup

```yaml
id: chicken-soup
meal_types: [lunch, dinner]
servings: 4
prep_minutes: 15
cook_minutes: 35
wait_minutes: 0
tags: [soup, comfort-food, one-pot]
```

A warming chicken soup with carrots, celery and rice, finished with parsley and a squeeze of lemon.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chicken | 400 g | Boneless, skinless chicken thighs | Soup |
| onion | 1 medium | Onion, finely chopped | Soup |
| carrots | 2 medium | Carrots, diced | Soup |
| celery | 2 sticks | Celery, diced | Soup |
| garlic | 2 cloves | Garlic, finely chopped | Soup |
| olive-oil | 1 tbsp | Olive oil | Soup |
| chicken-stock | 1.5 litres | Chicken stock | Soup |
| rice | 75 g | Long-grain white rice, rinsed | Soup |
| bay-leaf | 1 | Bay leaf | Seasoning |
| parsley | 1 small handful | Fresh parsley, chopped | To finish |
| lemon | Half | Lemon, juiced | To finish |
| salt | To taste | Salt | Seasoning |
| black-pepper | To taste | Ground black pepper | Seasoning |

### Method

1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan. Soften the onion, carrots and celery over medium heat for 8–10 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Pour in the stock, add the chicken and bay leaf, and bring to a gentle simmer.
3. Add the rice and simmer for 20–25 minutes, until the vegetables and rice are tender and the chicken reaches 75°C in the centre.
4. Lift the chicken onto a plate, shred with two forks and return it to the soup. Remove the bay leaf.
5. Stir in the parsley and lemon juice, season to taste and serve. Add a little hot water if you prefer a lighter broth.

### Notes

- The rice absorbs broth as the soup stands; add extra water or stock when reheating.

## Filled peppers

```yaml
id: filled-peppers
meal_types: [dinner, lunch]
servings: 4
prep_minutes: 25
cook_minutes: 70
wait_minutes: 5
tags: [stuffed-peppers, oven-baked, family-favourite]
```

Sweet peppers filled with seasoned beef and rice, baked in a tomato sauce until tender.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| peppers | 4 large | Bell peppers | Main |
| beef-mince | 400 g | Beef mince | Filling |
| rice | 150 g | Long-grain white rice, rinsed | Filling |
| onion | 1 medium | Onion, finely chopped | Filling |
| garlic | 2 cloves | Garlic, finely chopped | Filling |
| olive-oil | 1 tbsp | Olive oil | Filling |
| paprika | 1 tsp | Sweet paprika | Filling |
| oregano | 1 tsp | Dried oregano | Filling |
| chopped-tomatoes | 400 g | Tinned chopped tomatoes, divided | Filling and sauce |
| stock | 200 ml | Beef or vegetable stock | Sauce |
| parsley | 1 small handful | Fresh parsley, chopped | To finish |
| salt | To taste | Salt | Seasoning |
| black-pepper | To taste | Ground black pepper | Seasoning |

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C, or 180°C fan. Cook the rice in boiling water according to the packet, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, slice the tops off the peppers and remove the seeds and white membranes. Keep the tops as lids.
3. Heat the oil in a frying pan. Soften the onion for about 5 minutes, add the garlic for 1 minute, then add the mince. Cook for about 8 minutes until browned.
4. Stir in the paprika, oregano and half the tomatoes. Simmer for 5 minutes, then fold in the cooked rice and half the parsley. Season to taste.
5. Mix the remaining tomatoes and the stock in a deep baking dish. Stand the peppers in it, spoon the filling into them and replace their tops. Cover the dish tightly with foil.
6. Bake for 35 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for another 10–15 minutes, until the peppers are tender and the filling is piping hot.
7. Rest for 5 minutes, then spoon over the tomato sauce and scatter with the remaining parsley.

### Notes

- The rice is cooked before stuffing, so the filling can be spooned into the peppers without leaving room for rice to expand.
- Use a baking dish that holds the peppers upright and fairly snugly.

## Pancakes

```yaml
id: pancakes
meal_types: [breakfast]
servings: 4
prep_minutes: 10
cook_minutes: 20
wait_minutes: 0
tags: [vegetarian, family-favourite, quick]
```

Soft, fluffy pancakes for a relaxed breakfast. Serve with fresh fruit and a little honey or maple syrup.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| flour | 200 g | Self-raising flour | Batter |
| sugar | 1 tbsp | Caster sugar | Batter |
| salt | 1 pinch | Salt | Batter |
| egg | 1 large | Egg | Batter |
| milk | 250 ml | Milk | Batter |
| butter | 25 g | Butter, melted, plus a little extra for the pan | Batter and cooking |
| berries | 150 g | Fresh berries (optional) | To serve |
| syrup | 2 tbsp | Honey or maple syrup (optional) | To serve |

### Method

1. Mix the flour, sugar and salt in a bowl. In a jug, whisk the egg, milk and melted butter together.
2. Pour the wet ingredients into the flour mixture and whisk briefly until combined. A few small lumps are fine; avoid overmixing.
3. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and brush with a little butter. Add small ladlefuls of batter, leaving space between them.
4. Cook for about 2 minutes until bubbles appear on top and the edges begin to set. Flip and cook for another 1–2 minutes until golden and cooked through. Repeat with the remaining batter, adjusting the heat if they brown too quickly.
5. Serve warm with the berries and honey or maple syrup if using.

### Notes

- This quantity makes about 12 small pancakes, or 3 per serving.

## Macaroni bake

```yaml
id: macaroni-bake
meal_types: [breakfast]
servings: 4
prep_minutes: 10
cook_minutes: 45
wait_minutes: 10
tags: [vegetarian, sweet, oven-baked, make-ahead]
```

A gently sweet breakfast bake of macaroni, milk and eggs, with a golden top and a little vanilla. An optional sprinkle of feta adds a salty contrast.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| macaroni | 250 g | Dried macaroni | Pasta |
| butter | 20 g | Butter, divided between the dish and pasta | Pasta and dish |

| salt | 1 pinch | Salt | Custard |
| feta | 100 g | Feta, crumbled (optional) | Filling |

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 190°C, or 170°C fan. Grease a roughly 25 × 20 cm baking dish with a little of the butter.
2. Boil the macaroni for about 2 minutes less than the packet instructions, so it is still slightly firm. Drain, toss with the remaining butter and spread in the baking dish. Mix in the feta if using.
3. Whisk the eggs, milk, 50 g of the sugar, vanilla and salt together. Pour over the macaroni and gently stir so the custard reaches all the pasta.
4. Sprinkle the remaining 10 g of sugar over the top. Bake for 30–35 minutes until golden and the custard is set in the centre; allow a little longer if needed.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before cutting into portions. Serve warm.

### Notes

- This starter version is a sweet breakfast macaroni bake, rather than a savoury cheese pasta bake.
- The feta is optional; leave it out for a milder, sweeter version.

A Bulgarian-style chicken stew with onions, peppers and mushrooms in a tomato and white-wine sauce, seasoned with paprika and savoury herbs.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chicken | 600 g | Boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces | Stew |
| onions | 2 large | Onions, sliced | Stew |

| mushrooms | 250 g | Mushrooms, sliced | Stew |
| garlic | 2 cloves | Garlic, finely chopped | Stew |
| olive-oil | 2 tbsp | Olive oil | Stew |
| chopped-tomatoes | 400 g | Tinned chopped tomatoes | Sauce |
| white-wine | 150 ml | Dry white wine | Sauce |

| salt | To taste | Salt | Seasoning |
| black-pepper | To taste | Ground black pepper | Seasoning |

### Method

2. Lower the heat and soften the onions and peppers in the same casserole for about 8 minutes. Add the mushrooms and cook for another 5 minutes, then add the garlic for 1 minute.

## Wine Kebab

```yaml

```

Tender pork simmered with onions, red wine and tomato, served over fluffy rice. A comforting Bulgarian-style wine kebab with a rich sauce.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pork | 700 g | Boneless pork shoulder, cut into 2–3 cm cubes | Stew |
| onions | 2 large | Onions, finely chopped | Stew |
| garlic | 2 cloves | Garlic, finely chopped | Stew |
| olive-oil | 2 tbsp | Olive oil | Stew |
| flour | 1 tbsp | Plain flour | Sauce |
| tomato-puree | 2 tbsp | Tomato purée | Sauce |
| red-wine | 200 ml | Dry red wine | Sauce |
| stock | 350 ml | Beef or vegetable stock | Sauce |
| paprika | 1 tsp | Sweet paprika | Seasoning |
| bay-leaves | 2 | Bay leaves | Seasoning |
| salt | To taste | Salt | Seasoning |
| black-pepper | To taste | Ground black pepper | Seasoning |
| rice | 250 g | Long-grain white rice, rinsed | To serve |
| parsley | 1 small handful | Fresh parsley, chopped (optional) | To finish |

### Method

1. Pat the pork dry and season lightly. Heat the oil in a large casserole over medium-high heat and brown the pork in batches for about 10 minutes in total. Lift onto a plate.
2. Lower the heat and cook the onions in the same casserole for about 8 minutes until softened. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
3. Stir in the flour and tomato purée and cook for 1 minute. Add the paprika, then gradually stir in the wine, scraping up the browned bits. Let it bubble for 2–3 minutes.
4. Stir in the stock, add the bay leaves and return the pork and its juices to the casserole. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover and cook over a low heat for 75–90 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the pork is tender. Add a little hot water if needed to keep it covered in sauce.
5. During the final 15–20 minutes, cook the rice in water according to the packet instructions, then drain or let it absorb the water as directed.
6. Remove the bay leaves. If the sauce is too thin, simmer uncovered for a few minutes. Season to taste, spoon the pork and sauce over the rice and scatter with parsley if using.

### Notes

- This is a slow-cooked pork stew, rather than a kebab cooked on skewers. The rice is included in the ingredient quantities.
- Cooking time depends on the size of the pork pieces; continue simmering gently until they are tender.
