# Favourite recipes

This is the editable recipe collection for Top Recipes. The dish names are your starting favourites; the quantities and methods below are suggested starter versions, not a record of your personal recipes. Replace them freely with the versions you enjoy most.

To add a recipe, copy a complete recipe section from its `##` heading to the end of its notes, then change its title and permanent `id`. Keep the metadata fields, Ingredients table columns and Method heading. Use `breakfast`, `lunch` or `dinner` in `meal_types`; more than one is allowed. Keep ingredient IDs unique within each recipe. Optional ingredients should say “(optional)” in their name.

Each recipe's quantities make its stated number of servings. Times are estimates; `wait_minutes` includes unattended waiting such as chilling or resting. The website design is in [design.md](design.md).

## Overnight oats

```yaml
id: overnight-oats
meal_types: [breakfast]
servings: 1
prep_minutes: 5
cook_minutes: 0
wait_minutes: 480
tags: [make-ahead, no-cook, vegetarian]
```

Creamy oats prepared the night before, with yoghurt, berries and a little honey. A simple breakfast to take straight from the fridge.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rolled-oats | 40 g | Rolled oats | Oats |
| milk | 100 ml | Milk or unsweetened plant milk | Oats |
| yoghurt | 75 g | Plain Greek yoghurt | Oats |
| chia-seeds | 1 tsp | Chia seeds (optional) | Oats |
| honey | 1 tsp | Honey or maple syrup (optional) | Oats |
| berries | 80 g | Fresh or frozen berries | Topping |

### Method

1. Stir the oats, milk, yoghurt, chia seeds and honey together in a jar or covered container. Leave a little room for the oats to expand.
2. Cover and refrigerate for about 8 hours, or overnight.
3. Stir in the morning. Add a splash of milk if you prefer a looser texture, then top with the berries.

### Notes

- The starter timing includes an 8-hour overnight chill, so plan this breakfast the evening before.
- For a dairy-free version, use plant milk and plant yoghurt. Use maple syrup for a vegan version.
- Contains oats and, with the suggested dairy ingredients, milk. Use certified gluten-free oats if needed and check product labels.

## Chicken and leek pie

```yaml
id: chicken-and-leek-pie
meal_types: [lunch]
servings: 4
prep_minutes: 20
cook_minutes: 55
wait_minutes: 10
tags: [comfort-food, family-favourite, batch-cooking]
```

Tender chicken and softened leeks in a creamy sauce, finished with a golden puff-pastry lid. Serve with green vegetables or a simple salad.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chicken | 500 g | Boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces | Filling |
| leeks | 2 medium | Leeks, trimmed, washed and sliced | Filling |
| butter | 25 g | Butter | Filling |
| olive-oil | 1 tbsp | Olive oil | Filling |
| garlic | 2 cloves | Garlic, finely chopped | Filling |
| flour | 25 g | Plain flour | Filling |
| chicken-stock | 300 ml | Chicken stock | Filling |
| double-cream | 100 ml | Double cream | Filling |
| dijon-mustard | 1 tsp | Dijon mustard | Filling |
| thyme | 1 tsp | Dried thyme | Filling |
| salt | To taste | Salt | Seasoning |
| black-pepper | To taste | Ground black pepper | Seasoning |
| puff-pastry | 1 ready-rolled sheet, about 320 g | Puff pastry | Pastry lid |
| egg | 1 | Egg, beaten for brushing | Pastry lid |

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C, or 180°C fan. Have a roughly 24 cm pie dish ready.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Brown the chicken for about 6–8 minutes, working in batches if needed, then transfer to a plate. It will finish cooking in the sauce.
3. Lower the heat to medium, add the butter and leeks, and cook for about 8 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
4. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually add the stock, stirring to make a smooth sauce. Add the cream, mustard and thyme, then return the chicken and its juices to the pan.
5. Simmer gently for about 10 minutes until the sauce thickens and the chicken is cooked through. Check the largest pieces reach 75°C in the centre. Season to taste.
6. Transfer the filling to the pie dish and let it cool for about 10 minutes. Place the pastry over the dish, trim and crimp the edges, and cut a small steam vent in the centre. Brush with beaten egg.
7. Bake for 25–30 minutes until the pastry is puffed and golden and the filling is piping hot.

### Notes

- The 55-minute cooking estimate includes the hob and oven stages; actual time depends on the pan and oven.
- The filling can be prepared ahead, cooled promptly and refrigerated. If baking from a chilled filling, allow extra time and check the centre is piping hot before serving.
- Suggested sides are serving ideas and are not included in the ingredient quantities above.
- Contains wheat/gluten, milk, egg and mustard in the suggested ingredients; check stock and pastry labels too.

## Moussaka

```yaml
id: moussaka
meal_types: [dinner]
servings: 6
prep_minutes: 25
cook_minutes: 80
wait_minutes: 15
tags: [comfort-food, batch-cooking, weekend-cooking]
```

A comforting baked dish of roasted aubergine, a warmly spiced lamb and tomato sauce, and a creamy béchamel topping. This starter version uses aubergine without a potato layer.

### Ingredients

| ID | Quantity | Ingredient | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| aubergines | 3 large | Aubergines, sliced into rounds about 1 cm thick | Aubergine layer |
| olive-oil | 3 tbsp | Olive oil, divided between roasting and the sauce | Aubergine layer and sauce |
| lamb-mince | 500 g | Lamb mince | Meat sauce |
| onion | 1 large | Onion, finely chopped | Meat sauce |
| garlic | 2 cloves | Garlic, finely chopped | Meat sauce |
| chopped-tomatoes | 400 g | Tinned chopped tomatoes | Meat sauce |
| tomato-puree | 2 tbsp | Tomato purée | Meat sauce |
| cinnamon | 1 tsp | Ground cinnamon | Meat sauce |
| oregano | 1 tsp | Dried oregano | Meat sauce |
| butter | 50 g | Butter | Béchamel |
| flour | 50 g | Plain flour | Béchamel |
| milk | 600 ml | Milk | Béchamel |
| parmesan | 50 g | Parmesan, finely grated | Béchamel |
| egg | 1 | Egg, beaten | Béchamel |
| nutmeg | 1 pinch | Ground nutmeg | Béchamel |
| salt | To taste | Salt | Seasoning |
| black-pepper | To taste | Ground black pepper | Seasoning |

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C, or 200°C fan. Arrange the aubergine slices on baking trays, brush with 2 tablespoons of the olive oil, season lightly and roast for 25–30 minutes, turning once, until tender and lightly browned.
2. While the aubergine roasts, heat the remaining tablespoon of oil in a large pan. Cook the onion for about 5 minutes, add the garlic for 1 minute, then add the lamb. Cook for about 8 minutes, breaking it up until browned.
3. Stir in the tomato purée, cinnamon and oregano. Add the chopped tomatoes and simmer uncovered for about 20 minutes until thick, stirring occasionally. Season to taste.
4. Make the béchamel while the sauce simmers: melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the flour and cook for 1–2 minutes. Gradually whisk in the milk and cook for about 8 minutes until smooth and thickened.
5. Take the béchamel off the heat and let it cool for about 5 minutes. Stir in the Parmesan and nutmeg, then whisk in the beaten egg. Season lightly.
6. Reduce the oven to 200°C, or 180°C fan. In a roughly 30 × 20 cm baking dish, layer half the aubergine, the meat sauce and the remaining aubergine. Spread the béchamel over the top.
7. Bake for 35–40 minutes until the top is golden and the centre is piping hot. Rest for 15 minutes before cutting into portions.

### Notes

- The cooking estimate assumes the aubergine, meat sauce and béchamel are prepared in parallel, as described. Allow longer if making each component one after another.
- Beef mince can replace lamb if preferred.
- Contains wheat/gluten, milk and egg in the suggested ingredients.
