## Editorial update and evidence types

The page now leads with eight static headline panels: five principal funding/legal/trade questions and three panels on UC, energy and credible concessions. Every one of the 101 policy articles has an explicit evidence label describing the argument, not an automatic truth verdict. ## Source register

Each exact URL is retained. Findings state the purpose for which evidence is used; they should not be read as support for every sentence in a linked article. The articles add clearly reasoned economic inferences about incentives, distribution and implementation.

### energy-north-sea

- Publisher: House of Commons Library
- Title: North Sea oil and gas
- Publication/update date: 2026-09-29
- Exact URL: https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-12212/
- Accessed: 2026-10-27
- Supported finding / qualification: Describes declining production, the distinction between licences and development consent, the policy against new licences, and Rosebank's predominantly oil output. Reports January 2025 consents being effectively quashed and fresh applications in July 2026; this is dated evidence, not confirmation of today's final consent status.
