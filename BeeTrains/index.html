Planned milestone

A wider network, simpler connections

Further stations across the Airport, Stockport, Rochdale, Bolton and Wigan corridors are planned to join by December 2028. Integrated daily and weekly contactless caps across bus, tram and rail are also targeted by the end of 2028.

Rail-only contactless coverage and full Bee Network integration are separate milestones. Some local integration continues towards 2030.

Tickets, explained.

The goal is a simpler journey across train, tram and bus. Getting there involves different changes at different times.

Rail contactless, the daily paper ticket and integrated fare caps each have their own scope. Check the published rules before travelling.

Exact fares, concessions, Railcard treatment and cross-boundary validity need confirmation. Read the October ticketing update coverage.

Show an example

Illustrative journey: a train into Manchester, followed by a tram to your destination. A ticket covering both modes and a contactless cap are different ways of paying. This example describes the idea; it does not confirm a price or ticket’s validity.

A few useful answers.

The detail matters when you’re planning a real journey.

Will my train operator change?

Joining the Bee Network does not itself mean the Bee Network becomes the train operator. Existing train operating companies continue running services while TfGM works with government and the rail industry on integration. TfGM’s explanation.

Does joining mean my station will be step-free?

No. Station integration and accessibility projects have different delivery dates. Check the official station directory for current facilities before travelling.

Can I tap in for every train journey now?

This guide describes planned changes. The first rail contactless launch in the current programme is December 2026, followed by further rollout. Check TfGM’s latest notices for availability and ticketing rules.

Why do older reports say 17 stations?

Earlier announcements grouped 17 stations into the December 2026 launch. TfGM’s newer station list puts 14 in December 2026 and a further 15 in March 2027. This guide follows the newer list. See the current source.

Is this an official Bee Network website?

BeeTrains is an independent guide to published plans. Use TfGM, National Rail and your train operator for travel information, ticket conditions and current service updates.
