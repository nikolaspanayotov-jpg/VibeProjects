## Design and behavior

- **Reading tools and search:** a native disclosure after the opening briefing links to each tool and the two-page brief. The local search indexes visible and collapsed article passages, including source notes. It normalises punctuation/case, requires all query tokens, returns ten passages at a time and uses text-only rendering. Selecting a result opens ancestor disclosures and moves focus below the mobile header. Passage IDs are generated deterministically at initialisation so result links also work after reload. No query or preference is persisted or sent over the network. The no-JavaScript path keeps navigation and browser-Find guidance.
- **Valuation:** initial price $20 and 20bn economic shares are explicitly invented teaching inputs. Equity value in USD billions = price in dollars × economic shares in billions. Divide that result by selected revenue in USD billions. The default denominator is filed January–September 2026 revenue of $33.242bn; it is not annualised. An explicit alternative uses user-assumed 12-month revenue (initial example $50bn), with no claim of company guidance. The nine sensitivity cells vary price by −20/0/+20% and shares by −10/0/+10%; revenue stays fixed. This is equity value, not enterprise value, and does not model issuance proceeds. Invalid fields hide stale results. Tiny positive outputs display below the rounding threshold rather than as zero. Reset restores the original example. Final offer terms remain unresolved in the reviewed snapshot.
- **Evidence tracker:** replaces the prior three short scenario cards with dated evidence, analytical status, strengthening/weakening signals and next disclosures. All three remain conditional, without probabilities or investment ratings. A signal strengthening the downside scenario is not labelled a tailwind. Financial periods and operating cash flow versus investment remain explicit.
- **Partner map:** selected Amazon/AWS, Google, Microsoft/Azure, Broadcom and SpaceX/SpaceXAI relationships use native disclosures. Roles remain visible; opening a relationship reveals filed evidence, analytical benefit/dependency and sources. Expanded cards use the full row. Lines show association, not payment size or ownership. Cloud partners are also customers. This is a selected, non-exhaustive map.
- **Two-page brief:** `investment-brief.html` is a separate standalone document with embedded existing fonts, mobile layouts, ordinary evidence links, browser print/save-PDF control and a no-JavaScript print path. A4 print at 100% scale has two pages: investment case and key evidence; unresolved terms, catalysts and sources. It retains the 5 November research snapshot, 2 November filing, 4 November event-schedule review and historical 29 October SpaceX endpoint. No hypothetical calculator input appears as an actual valuation in the brief.
