## Implementation decisions

Preserve all financial, commitment and SpaceX datasets, stable anchors, the existing reported-nine-month valuation default and UK entry-funding calculator. Add the five requested content anchors, a separate forward matrix, a distinct round-trip planner, eight dated source previews and six glossary explanations. Preserve no persistence and no runtime requests.

The forward illustration uses an assumed year-end 2027 valuation date, 2028 revenue, author-selected revenue/multiple combinations and 18.012993379bn economic shares as a sensitivity denominator. Show all nine combinations and separately controlled dilution. Do not equate this denominator with Morningstar's, or change its published $92 to the approximately $94.38 quotient of rounded equity value divided by our shares. Fixed-equity dilution isolates share-count effects and assumes no value from new cash. The original valuation calculator remains separate.

The public position example starts with 20 × $74, $2,072 USD purchase/holding cash cap, 30-share cap and invented USD/GBP 1.25. Optional additions and sales are off. Reference value $92 is an assumed mark; no sale is executed by the model. Charges start **not supplied**, with an explicit before-costs mode. Entering all active charges, including explicit zeros, is necessary for the with-costs mode. Neither broker's tariff is silently assumed.

Process initial purchase, at most two optional purchases, then at most three sales; no new purchases after that sale cycle. Each optional purchase is limited to five shares, scaled down to floor(initial quantity / 4) for a smaller allocation, and has a distinct review number. Price changes never enable purchases. Zero allocation leaves the position empty until a new initial-entry decision; there is no division by zero. Disallow short sales. Cash/share breaches remain visible with the arithmetic, not disguised as valid within-cap plans.

USD- and GBP-denominated costs are editable separately. To include GBP fees in the USD economic ledger, translate them at that lot's USD-per-GBP assumption; label USD totals as USD-equivalent economic amounts. GBP purchase/sale cash flows retain each lot's own FX rate. Allocate acquisition costs proportionately across sales. Combined P/L subtracts total purchase costs only once. Holding charges remain unallocated and are subtracted once; GBP holding charges translate at the assumed mark FX for the USD comparison. Fully sold average/break-even is not applicable.

The contribution cap covers gross acquisition contributions, holding charges and any sale cash shortfall if charges exceed proceeds; sale receipts do not replenish the cap. It is distinct from market exposure, which can rise above the original cash cap. Compare USD totals only with a USD cap and GBP totals only with a GBP cap. Changing FX never automatically enlarges a sterling cap. Zero-quantity lots incur no modelled dealing fees; other failed-application charges can be entered as holding/other costs.

The downside comparison is a separate pre-sale acquisition-path illustration, not an executed strategy. At $40, the unchanged 20-share example loses $680; both five-share additions at $62.90/$51.80 lose $853.50 despite reducing average cost. The $78 entry discipline, 85%/70% review levels, +25%/+50% sale references and 40% drawdown review are author-selected rules, not broker orders, price targets or loss guarantees. A cash subscription does not guarantee both quantity and price caps.
